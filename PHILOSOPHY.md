# Why it's built this way

This started as curiosity: EMDR sounded interesting, and it was surprising to learn how much of it, like following a
moving object, rating how you feel, picturing a calm place, and grounding at the end, is described openly in published
research. This is a small, simple tool for trying those pieces out gently, on your own computer.

A few choices follow from that:

- **Keep it small.** One download, no installer. Run it, use it, delete it when you're done.
- **Keep it private.** No account and no internet connection. Your ratings stay in a file on your own computer, and you
  can delete them from inside the app.
- **Be clear about what it is not.** Not therapy, not EMDR, not reviewed by a clinician. Real care comes from trained
  people, whether in person or online, and this tool is meant to sit alongside that, not replace it.
- **Keep it open.** MIT licensed, so anyone can read every line the guide says, change it, or build on it.

Most important: if something is weighing on you, please talk to a therapist, a doctor, or someone you trust. In the US
you can call or text **988**; elsewhere, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
